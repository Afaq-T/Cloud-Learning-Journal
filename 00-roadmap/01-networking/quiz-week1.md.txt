### 6. What is the difference between a hub and a switch?
**Your Answer:** Hub direct connection, switch multiple connections  
**Correct Answer:**  
- **Hub:** Layer 1 device, broadcasts data to all ports, no MAC address table, inefficient.  
- **Switch:** Layer 2 device, uses MAC address table to forward data only to the intended device, efficient and secure.  
**Result:** ❌ Incorrect (your explanation was inaccurate)

### 7. Give an example of a private IP address range (one of the three).
**Your Answer:** 192.168.0.1

- 172.16.0.0 – 172.31.255.255 (172.16.0.0/12)  
- 192.168.0.0 – 192.168.255.255 (192.168.0.0/16)  
You gave an example IP, not the range.  
**Result:** ⚠️ Partially Correct

### 8. What does CIDR notation `/24` mean in terms of subnet mask and number of addresses?
**Your Answer:** 24 bits network, 8 bits remaining, 254 hosts  
**Correct Answer:** /24 means subnet mask 255.255.255.0; total 256 addresses, 254 usable hosts (first is network, last is broadcast).  
**Result:** ⚠️ Partially Correct (your explanation about "network host full and user host not connected" was unclear)
